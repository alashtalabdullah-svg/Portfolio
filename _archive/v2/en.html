About Me (&)
My Journey

I started as an administrative assistant in the import sector and grew into leading operations and quality. In between: five years of building systems inside environments that had none.

’20
Hermony ImportAugust 2020

Starting out in import

I joined Hermony Import & Export Group as an administrative assistant in the import department — the link between the executive director and the team.

My job was to make sure information arrived before anyone had to ask for it: escalating updates to management, handling correspondence with international partners, and sourcing new suppliers. That is where I learned that most delays don't come from a lack of effort — they come from the absence of a clear path for information.

’23
Zurbian IslandMay 2023

Building from zero

Administrative supervision at Zurbian Island Restaurant, alongside my group responsibilities. I took part in setting the operation up from nothing.

I built the manual schedules and systems for tracking expenses and inventory before the shift to digital. Starting on paper was deliberate: a system that doesn't work on paper won't start working simply because you digitised it.

’24
Restaurant & Operations ManagerMay 2024

Full ownership

I took direct charge of the restaurant and its operations, leading a team of ten people.

The focus was cost control, waste reduction and stronger internal oversight. I used AI tools to analyse operational performance results, and defined each person's responsibilities clearly so the work never overlapped.

’25
Professional certificates2023 — 2025

Investing in the craft

I completed the McKinsey Forward soft-skills development programme, after two digital marketing certificates from Global TAG.

Alongside those: an internal training certificate from Exceed Center, and climate action & sustainability training from the National Forum for Environment. I picked each one because it closed a real gap I had hit in the field.

’26
EduStation · University of Modern SciencesMarch 2026 — present

Leading operations & quality

I moved to EduStation in Dammam as Operations & Quality Lead, and graduated with a Bachelor's in International Business Administration.

The current role brings together everything that came before it: building procedures, verifying output against standards, and coordinating across departments. The degree from the University of Modern Sciences closed the theoretical side of what I had been building in practice since 2020.
